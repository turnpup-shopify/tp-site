# Fancy Tuna Sandwich

Chef Meherwan Irani's tuna sandwich. Oil packed tuna, good mayo, good olive oil, sweet and sour pickles, pickled jalapeños and a lot of cracked black pepper on lightly toasted seeded sourdough.

The rule is simple. Buy the best version of each ingredient. There are only eight of them.

## Ingredients

### Tuna salad

- 1 (5 to 7 oz) jar or can oil packed tuna, drained
- 2 tbsp mayo (Hellmann's)
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil (Greek)
- 2 tbsp onion, finely diced
- 2 tbsp sweet and sour pickles, finely diced
- 1 tbsp pickled jalapeños, finely diced
- Freshly cracked black pepper, a lot
- Pinch of salt, only if needed

### Bread

- 4 slices seeded sourdough

## Instructions

### 1. Dice the crunch

Finely dice the onion, pickles and jalapeños. Small enough that you get some of each in every bite.

### 2. Mix the tuna

Drain the tuna and flake it into a bowl. Add the mayo, olive oil, onion, pickles and jalapeños. Mix gently. Crack in a generous amount of black pepper and mix again. Taste. Add salt only if it needs it. Tuna and pickles are already salty.

### 3. Toast the bread

Lightly toast the sourdough. You want it warm with a little crunch, not hard.

### 4. Assemble

Pile the tuna on two slices. More pepper on top. Close with the other two slices. Cut and eat.

## Notes

### Brands Irani calls out

- Tuna. Ortiz, Wild Planet, Bela or Tonnino. Oil packed, not water packed.
- Olive oil. Greek.
- Mayo. Hellmann's.
- Pickles. The Backyard Food Company Czech Sweet and Sour Heirloom Pickle. https://thebackyardfoodcompany.com/czech-sweet-sour-pickle

### Flavor map

- Oil packed tuna is richer and softer than water packed. The olive oil doubles down on that.
- Sweet and sour pickles bring both sugar and acid. A plain dill pickle only brings acid.
- Pickled jalapeños add heat plus a second acid.
- Raw onion is the crunch and the bite.
- Black pepper is the finishing spice. Do not skimp.

### Kitchen reminders

- Dice everything small. Big chunks fall out of the sandwich.
- Toast lightly. Over toasted bread shreds the roof of your mouth and fights the soft filling.
- Mix the tuna gently so it stays flaky, not pasty.
