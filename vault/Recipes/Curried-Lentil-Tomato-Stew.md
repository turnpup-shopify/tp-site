# Curried Lentil Tomato Stew

Red lentils simmered in curry spiced coconut milk and crushed tomatoes. Couscous goes in at the end and soaks up the broth so it eats like a thick stew. Spinach and lime to finish. One pot.

## Ingredients

### Aromatics

- 2 tbsp virgin coconut oil or extra virgin olive oil
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 2 1/2 inch piece ginger, peeled and finely grated
- 1 tbsp medium curry powder
- 1 tsp smoked pepper flakes

### Stew

- 3/4 cup red lentils, rinsed well
- 1 (14.5 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 (13.5 oz) can unsweetened coconut milk
- 4 cups veggie broth, divided
- 1 cup plain couscous, uncooked
- Kosher salt and freshly ground pepper

### To finish

- Handful of spinach
- Lime wedges

## Instructions

### 1. Brown the onion

Heat the oil in a medium saucepan over medium. Cook the onion, stirring often, 8 to 10 minutes until softened and golden brown.

### 2. Bloom the spices

Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder and smoked pepper flakes. Cook, stirring, about 2 minutes until fragrant.

### 3. Toast the lentils

Rinse the lentils in a fine mesh sieve under cold water until the water runs mostly clear. Drain well. Add to the pot and cook, stirring, 1 minute.

### 4. Simmer

Stir in the tomatoes, a generous pinch of salt and 2 1/2 cups of the broth. Season with pepper. Add the coconut milk. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer gently, stirring now and then, about 15 minutes until the lentils are just tender.

### 5. Add the couscous

Stir in the couscous and the remaining 1 1/2 cups broth. Cover and simmer 5 minutes. Turn off the heat and let sit covered another 5 minutes so the couscous steams and fluffs.

### 6. Finish

Stir in the spinach until wilted. Taste. Add salt and pepper if needed. Serve with lime wedges.

## Notes

### Flavor map

- Browning the onion all the way to golden is where the sweetness comes from. Do not rush it.
- Curry powder and ginger are the warmth. Smoked pepper flakes add heat plus a little smoke.
- Coconut milk makes it rich and rounds the acid from the tomatoes.
- Red lentils break down and thicken the stew on their own. No blending needed.
- Lime at the end wakes everything up. Do not skip it.

### Kitchen reminders

- Red lentils cook fast and go mushy. Pull them at just tender since they keep cooking with the couscous.
- Broth is split. 2 1/2 cups with the lentils, 1 1/2 cups with the couscous.
- This thickens a lot as it sits. Loosen leftovers with a splash of broth or water.
