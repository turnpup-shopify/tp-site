# Crispy Haloumi with Honey and Pistachio

Salty haloumi seared until golden, then hit with honey, crushed pistachios and flaky salt. Four ingredients. Ten minutes. Serve warm.

## Ingredients

### Haloumi

- 8 oz haloumi, cut into 1/2 inch slabs

### To finish

- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp toasted pistachios, finely chopped
- Flaky sea salt

## Instructions

### 1. Sear the haloumi

Heat a nonstick skillet over medium high. No oil. Lay in the haloumi slabs and sear 3 to 5 minutes per side until golden brown. Work in batches so the slabs are not touching.

### 2. Finish and serve

Plate the haloumi. Drizzle with honey. Sprinkle with pistachios and flaky sea salt. Serve warm.

## Notes

### Flavor map

- Haloumi is very salty and squeaky. High heat gives it a crust while the inside stays soft.
- Honey is the sweet against the salt. The pairing is the whole dish.
- Pistachios add crunch and a little bitterness.
- Flaky salt on top of an already salty cheese sounds wrong. It works. It is about texture and the sharp hit on the tongue.

### Kitchen reminders

- No oil. Haloumi has enough fat to brown on its own in a nonstick pan.
- Pat the slabs dry first if they are wet from the brine. Water stops browning.
- Eat it right away. Haloumi goes rubbery as it cools.
- Toast raw pistachios in a dry pan 2 to 3 minutes if you only have raw.
