# Roasted Carrots with Dates, Pistachios and Tahini Yogurt

Carrots roasted until the edges caramelize, tossed hot with chopped dates, pistachios and pepperoncini, then piled on lemon tahini yogurt. Sweet, salty, sour and crunchy on one plate.

Pairs with [[Lemon Oregano Salmon]]. Both roast at 425°F so they share the oven.

## Ingredients

### Roasted carrots

- Carrots, sliced diagonally into 1/2 inch pieces (see Notes for amount)
- 12 g olive oil
- 1/2 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

### Date pistachio topping

- 30 g Medjool dates, finely chopped
- 25 g roasted unsalted pistachios, roughly chopped
- 2 pepperoncini peppers, finely chopped
- 15 g lemon juice
- 10 g olive oil
- 1/8 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt

### Lemon tahini yogurt

- 80 g plain Greek yogurt
- 20 g tahini
- 10 g lemon juice
- 1/8 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- 1 to 2 tsp cold water, only if needed

## Instructions

### 1. Roast the carrots

Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss the carrots with olive oil, salt and pepper. Spread on a baking sheet in a single layer. Roast 15 to 18 minutes, flip, then roast another 10 to 17 minutes. 25 to 35 minutes total, until tender with caramelized edges.

### 2. Make the topping

While the carrots roast, combine the dates, pistachios, pepperoncini, lemon juice, olive oil and salt in a bowl. Mix and set aside.

### 3. Make the yogurt

Whisk the yogurt, tahini, lemon juice and salt until smooth. Add cold water a teaspoon at a time only if needed for a creamy, spreadable consistency. Refrigerate.

### 4. Finish the carrots

The moment the carrots come out of the oven, add the date pistachio topping directly to the hot baking sheet. Toss gently to coat. Do not return to the oven.

### 5. Plate

Spread half the lemon tahini yogurt on each plate. Spoon the warm carrots and topping over the yogurt.

## Notes

### Flavor map

- Roasting concentrates the carrot sugar. The caramelized edges are the point. Do not pull them early.
- Dates double down on sweet. Pepperoncini and lemon pull it back with sour and a little heat.
- Pistachios are the crunch.
- Tahini yogurt is cool, rich and a little bitter. It is the base everything sits on.
- Tossing the topping on the hot pan warms the dates and lets the oil pick up the browned bits.

### Kitchen reminders

- Single layer on the pan. Crowded carrots steam and go soft without browning.
- Yogurt sauce can be made a day ahead.
- Diamond Crystal is lighter than Morton. If using Morton kosher, use about half.
- Weights given in grams. 12 g olive oil is about 1 tbsp. 80 g yogurt is about 1/3 cup. 20 g tahini is about 1 tbsp. 30 g dates is about 2 Medjool dates.
