# Ground Chicken Rice Bowls with Water Chestnuts and Mushrooms

Crumbly browned chicken, a pile of vegetables and crunchy water chestnuts in a glossy soy, honey and ginger sauce. Served over rice. One skillet.

Makes a big batch. Good for meal prep.

## Ingredients

### Rice

- 1 cup rice

### Chicken and vegetables

- 2 lbs ground chicken
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- Pinch of salt
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 1/2 tbsp fresh ginger, grated (or 2 tsp ground ginger)
- 6 green onions, sliced
- 2 medium carrots, shredded or julienned
- 2 red bell peppers, diced
- 2 cups mushrooms, finely chopped
- 1 can (8 oz) water chestnuts, drained and diced

### Sauce

- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp honey (or brown sugar)
- 3 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 to 3 tsp chili crisp, sriracha or red pepper flakes, to taste

### To serve

- Lime wedges, optional

## Instructions

### 1. Cook the rice

Cook 1 cup rice however you normally do. Keep warm.

### 2. Mix the sauce

Whisk the soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, sesame oil and chili in a bowl. Taste and adjust.

### 3. Brown the chicken

Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium high. Add the chicken with a pinch of salt. Break it apart and cook 8 to 10 minutes until browned and crumbly with no pink left.

### 4. Add the aromatics

Add the garlic, ginger and green onions. Cook 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant.

### 5. Cook the vegetables

Add the mushrooms, carrots and bell peppers. Cook 5 to 6 minutes until softened and the mushrooms have released their liquid.

### 6. Add the water chestnuts

Stir in the water chestnuts. Cook 1 minute to warm through. Keep them crunchy.

### 7. Sauce it

Pour the sauce over everything. Stir and simmer 3 to 4 minutes until glossy and coated.

### 8. Serve

Spoon over rice. Squeeze of lime if you want it.

## Notes

### Flavor map

- Soy sauce is the salt and the savory base.
- Honey balances the salt and helps the sauce go glossy.
- Rice vinegar lifts it so it does not taste flat or heavy.
- Sesame oil is for aroma. A little goes a long way.
- Water chestnuts are there for texture only. They taste of almost nothing. The crunch is the point.

### Kitchen reminders

- Chop the mushrooms fine so they blend into the chicken instead of sitting on top as chunks.
- Do not crowd the pan when browning. If the chicken steams instead of browning, cook it in two batches.
- Fresh ginger grates easiest frozen. Scrape the skin off with a spoon first.
