# Lemon Oregano Salmon

Salmon roasted skin side down under a lemon, garlic and oregano oil. Fifteen minutes in the oven. Finish with fresh lemon.

Pairs with [[Roasted Carrots with Dates, Pistachios and Tahini Yogurt]]. Both roast at 425°F so they share the oven.

## Ingredients

### Salmon

- 1 lb salmon
- Lemon wedges for serving

### Lemon oregano oil

- 15 g olive oil
- 15 g lemon juice
- 4 g garlic, grated
- 3/4 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 g lemon zest

## Instructions

### 1. Preheat

Preheat oven to 425°F.

### 2. Mix the oil

Stir together the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, pepper and lemon zest.

### 3. Prep the salmon

Pat the salmon dry. Place skin side down on a parchment lined baking sheet. Spoon the oil mixture evenly over the fillets.

### 4. Roast

Roast 12 to 16 minutes until the internal temperature reaches 145°F.

### 5. Serve

Finish with a squeeze of fresh lemon.

## Notes

### Flavor map

- Lemon juice in the oil, zest on top. Juice is the acid, zest is the aroma.
- Dried oregano is the Greek note. It holds up to the oven better than fresh.
- Grated garlic spreads evenly and does not burn the way slices would.

### Kitchen reminders

- Pat the fish dry or the oil slides off and the surface steams instead of roasts.
- 145°F is the USDA target and gives firm, fully cooked salmon. Pull at 125°F to 130°F if you like it medium and a little translucent in the center.
- Diamond Crystal is lighter than Morton. If using Morton kosher, use about half.
- Weights given in grams. 15 g olive oil is about 1 tbsp. 15 g lemon juice is about 1 tbsp. 4 g garlic is about 1 clove.
