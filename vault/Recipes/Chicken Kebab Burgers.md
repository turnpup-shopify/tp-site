# Chicken Kebab Burgers with Mint Cilantro Yogurt

Chapli style ground chicken patties on soft buns with a cool herb yogurt. Tart anardana, warm coriander and cumin, a sharp edge of ajwain.

Yum

## Ingredients

### Chicken and aromatics

- 1 lb ground chicken breast (99% lean)
- 6 g fresh garlic, finely grated (about 2 cloves)
- 7 g fresh ginger, finely grated (a thumb sized knob)
- 1/4 cup red onion, very finely diced
- 1/4 cup Roma tomato, seeded and finely diced (about 1/2 tomato)
- 1 small green chili, finely minced (remove seeds for less heat)
- 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh mint, finely chopped
- 1 large egg
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (mixed into the chicken)

### Spices

- 1 tsp smoky chili flakes
- 1 1/2 tsp whole coriander seeds
- 1 tsp whole cumin seeds
- 2 1/2 tsp anardana powder (about 6 g)
- 1/4 tsp ajwain seeds
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 tsp fine salt (about 6 g)

### For cooking

- 1 to 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1/2 lemon

### Mint cilantro yogurt sauce

- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro
- 1 tbsp fresh mint
- 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
- 1/2 small green chili (optional)
- Pinch of salt
- 1 to 2 tsp water, only if needed

### For the burgers

- 4 soft burger buns
- Thinly sliced red onion
- Thinly sliced cucumber
- Roma tomato slices
- Optional tamarind chutney

## Instructions

### 1. Toast and crush the spices

Preheat a dry skillet over medium low for about a minute. Stainless works fine.

Add the coriander seeds, cumin seeds and ajwain seeds. Toast 30 to 60 seconds, stirring constantly, until fragrant. Ajwain burns fast. Pull them the moment they smell nutty.

Tip into a mortar and let cool a minute. Crush coarsely. A few turns of the pestle. You want visible cracked pieces, not powder.

Keep the anardana powder separate.

### 2. Prepare the vegetables

Finely dice the red onion, Roma tomato, green chili, cilantro and mint.

Remove the seeds and watery center of the tomato first so the chicken mix does not get wet.

Grate the garlic and ginger on a microplane. For ginger, scrape the skin off with the edge of a spoon, slice a piece off the end, and grate that cut face so you cut across the fibers.

### 3. Mix the kebabs

In a large bowl combine

- Ground chicken
- Garlic and ginger
- Toasted crushed spices
- Anardana, smoky chili flakes, black pepper and salt
- Red onion, tomato, chili, cilantro and mint
- Egg
- 1 tbsp neutral oil

Mix gently with your hands until just combined. Stop as soon as it looks even. Overmixing makes the chicken dense.

### 4. Chill

Cover the bowl with plastic wrap or a plate. Refrigerate 20 to 30 minutes.

### 5. Make the yogurt sauce

Put the Greek yogurt in a bowl. Finely chop the cilantro and mint and stir them in. Add the lemon juice, a pinch of salt, and the green chili if using.

Stir and taste. It should be thick and spreadable like tzatziki, not pourable. Only add water a teaspoon at a time if it will not spread. Keep cold.

### 6. Shape and cook the patties

Divide the mix into 4 patties, a bit wider than the buns and about 3/4 inch thick. Oil your hands first since lean chicken sticks. Press a shallow dimple in the center so they do not dome.

Heat 1 to 2 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium high until it shimmers. Lay in the patties and leave them alone for 4 minutes to crust. Flip. Cook 4 to 5 more minutes until the center reads 165°F or juices run clear.

Squeeze the lemon over the patties in the last minute. Rest 2 minutes.

### 7. Assemble

Toast the buns lightly. Spread yogurt sauce on both halves. Add a patty, then sliced red onion, cucumber and tomato. Tamarind chutney if you like. Serve right away.

## Notes

### Swaps that worked

- No green chili. Add 1/4 tsp extra smoky chili flakes to the chicken mix (1 1/4 tsp total). Or half a jalapeño, minced.
- A tablespoon of finely minced red onion in the yogurt sauce adds crunch, like a raita. Rinse it under cold water first to soften the raw edge. Skip tomato in the sauce, it goes watery.

### Flavor map

- Coriander is citrusy and floral. It is the seed of the cilantro plant.
- Cumin is earthy and smoky.
- Anardana is dried ground pomegranate seed. Tart and fruity. It brightens the warm spices and tenderizes the lean chicken.
- Ajwain is sharp and thyme like.
- Cilantro and mint in the sauce cover different notes. Mint is cool and sweet, cilantro is bright and citrusy. Together they cool the heat from the patty.

### Kitchen reminders

- 3 teaspoons in a tablespoon.
- Neutral oil means canola, vegetable, grapeseed, avocado or light olive oil. Not extra virgin olive or sesame.
- Freeze leftover ginger. It grates much easier frozen.
