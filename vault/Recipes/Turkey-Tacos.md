# The Best Turkey Tacos

Stovetop taco meat that stays juicy. Tomato sauce, a splash of vinegar and a pinch of brown sugar keep lean turkey from drying out. One pan, 20 minutes.

Makes about 4 cups, 1 cup per serving.

## Ingredients

### Aromatics

- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp taco seasoning (one store bought packet or homemade, see Notes)
- 1 tsp dried oregano

### Meat and sauce

- 1 lb ground turkey
- 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
- 2 tsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tsp packed brown sugar
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### To serve

- Tortillas
- Optional toppings. Shredded cheese, lettuce, diced tomato, diced avocado, sour cream.

## Instructions

### 1. Cook the onion

Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium high until it shimmers. Add the onion and cook about 5 minutes until soft.

### 2. Bloom the seasoning

Stir in the taco seasoning, garlic and oregano. Cook about 30 seconds until fragrant.

### 3. Brown the turkey

Add the ground turkey. Cook, stirring occasionally and breaking it up, about 2 minutes until nearly cooked through but still a little pink.

### 4. Simmer the sauce

Stir in the tomato sauce, vinegar and brown sugar. Simmer about 4 minutes until thickened. Season with salt and pepper.

### 5. Serve

Spoon into warm tortillas. Add your toppings. Serve right away.

## Notes

### Homemade taco seasoning

Makes about 2 tbsp, enough for this recipe.

- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 3/4 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- About 1/2 tsp salt, to taste
- Optional 1 tsp cornstarch if you want the sauce to thicken more

### Flavor map

- Tomato sauce adds moisture and body. This is what keeps lean turkey from going dry and crumbly.
- Apple cider vinegar brightens the sauce.
- Brown sugar rounds the acid and adds a little depth.
- Oregano is the herb note in most taco seasoning blends. Extra here makes it read more clearly.

### Kitchen reminders

- Turkey goes in slightly underdone. It finishes cooking in the sauce during the simmer.
- Taste before salting. Packet seasoning is already salty.
