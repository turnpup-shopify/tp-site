# Pickled Sweet Peppers

Quick refrigerator pickle. Sweet peppers in a mild rice vinegar brine with raw garlic. Ready in an hour. No canning.

Good on sandwiches, tacos, rice bowls and eggs.

## Ingredients

### Peppers

- 2 cups sliced sweet peppers
- 4 garlic cloves, minced

### Brine

- 1/4 cup rice vinegar
- 3/4 cup water
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tsp salt

## Instructions

### 1. Pack the jar

Put the sliced peppers in a clean jar.

### 2. Make the brine

Heat the vinegar, water, sugar and salt in a small pot. Stir until the sugar and salt dissolve. Remove from heat and stir in the garlic.

### 3. Pour over

Pour the brine over the peppers. If the peppers are not fully covered, top off with a little extra water.

### 4. Chill

Cover the jar and refrigerate at least 1 hour before serving.

## Notes

### Flavor map

- Rice vinegar is mild and a little sweet. This brine is gentle, not sharp.
- Sugar and salt in near equal weight make it sweet and sour rather than purely sour.
- Garlic goes in off heat so it stays punchy instead of cooking down.

### Kitchen reminders

- Refrigerator pickle only. Not shelf stable. Keep it cold.
- Slice peppers thin for fast pickling. Thicker slices need longer, overnight is better.
- Any sweet pepper works. Mini sweet peppers, bell peppers, banana peppers.
- Reuse the brine once for a second batch of peppers or sliced red onion.
