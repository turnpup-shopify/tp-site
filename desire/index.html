Is it a real desire?

Sheldon & Elliot sort the reasons people chase a goal from controlled to autonomous: external regulation (rewards, punishments), introjected regulation (guilt, shame), identified regulation (values), and intrinsic enjoyment. Reaching a controlled goal does little for you: those motives aren't linked to need satisfaction, so hitting them barely moves well-being. (SDT)
